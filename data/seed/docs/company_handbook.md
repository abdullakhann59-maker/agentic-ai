# Acme Retail Pvt Ltd - Employee Handbook (Demo)

## Leave policy
Employees get 24 days of paid leave per year. Up to 8 unused days can be carried forward to the next year.
Sick leave of more than 2 days needs a doctor's note. Leave requests must be raised in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance.

## Work from home
Employees can work from home 2 days per week after completing 3 months in the company. Team leads approve the schedule every Monday.

## Expense reimbursement
Travel expenses are reimbursed within 15 working days after the claim is approved. Claims above INR 10,000 need approval from the department head. Original bills must be uploaded with every claim.

## Sales incentives
Sales executives earn 5% incentive on monthly revenue above their target. Team leads earn an additional 1% on total team revenue when the team reaches 100% of its target.
